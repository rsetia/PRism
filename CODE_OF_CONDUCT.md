# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Prism aims to be a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free project for
everyone. Participants are expected to treat one another with respect,
regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, appearance, or
viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Healthy participation includes:

- discussing ideas and code without attacking people;
- giving specific, actionable, and considerate feedback;
- accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes;
- respecting different experiences and levels of familiarity;
- protecting private information and reporting sensitive matters privately;
- prioritizing the long-term health of the project over winning an argument.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, personal or political attacks, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without
permission.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository activity and other spaces where someone is
officially representing Prism. It also applies to private communication that
directly concerns project participation.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **ravpreetsetia@gmail.com**. Include
relevant links or context when safe to do so. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, reviewed fairly, and shared only as needed to
investigate or protect participants.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict
participation, or issue temporary or permanent bans. The response will be
proportionate to the behavior, its impact, prior incidents, and participant
safety. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is
itself a violation.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the principles and enforcement model of the

2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
