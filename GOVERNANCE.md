# Governance

Prism is currently a maintainer-led project.

## Roles

- **Contributors** report problems, propose changes, improve documentation,
  review work, or submit code.
- **Reviewers and triagers** are contributors trusted to help assess issues and
  pull requests. This role does not imply release or repository authority.
- **Maintainers** merge changes, make releases, manage security and conduct
  reports, and decide when consensus cannot be reached.

Ravpreet Setia is the current maintainer and final decision-maker.

## Decision-making

Routine fixes are decided through pull-request review. Material API,
persistence, schema, security, dependency, or architectural changes should
begin with an issue and may require an architecture decision record.

The project seeks evidence-based consensus: compatibility, safety, maintenance
cost, testability, and fit with Prism's explicit-port architecture matter more
than vote counts. When reasonable contributors disagree, the maintainer will
document the decision and its tradeoffs.

Decisions may be revisited when new evidence or project needs emerge.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer access is based on sustained, high-quality participation; sound
technical judgment; respectful collaboration; and willingness to perform
review and maintenance work. Existing maintainers appoint new maintainers and
may narrow or remove access when responsibilities change.

As the maintainer group grows, this document should be updated before the
decision process changes.

## Project administration

Releases and repository settings require maintainer approval. Security reports
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and community moderation follows
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Prism is maintained without a guaranteed response time. Silence is not
acceptance of a proposal, and contributors should not assume that an
unreviewed change will be merged.
